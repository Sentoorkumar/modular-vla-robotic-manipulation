# ============================================================
# FEEDBACK TEMPLATES - Pfand Sorting System
# ============================================================
# Purpose: Standard response templates for user communication.
# Usage:   Replace placeholders {object}, {count}, {bin},
#          {description}, {reason} with actual values.
# ============================================================

## SUCCESS TEMPLATES
- pick_success: "I have picked the {object}."
- sort_success: "Sorted {count} {object}(s) to Bin {bin}."
- place_success: "Placed the {object} in Bin {bin}."
- complete_success: "Task completed. {summary}"
- sort_group_done: "All {object}s sorted to Bin {bin}. Moving to next type."
- clear_complete: "Table cleared. Sorted {bottle_count} bottle(s) to Bin A and {can_count} can(s) to Bin B."

## FAILURE TEMPLATES
- object_not_found: "I cannot see any {object}(s) on the table."
- object_unreachable: "The {object} is outside my reachable workspace."
- unsafe_position: "The {object} is too close to the edge. It is unsafe to pick."
- no_vision: "I cannot see the table right now. Please check the camera."
- empty_table: "The table is empty. There is nothing to sort."

## CLARIFICATION TEMPLATES
- ambiguous_object: "I see {count} {object}(s). Which one do you mean?"
- ambiguous_type: "Do you mean the bottles, the cans, or all of them?"
- ambiguous_command: "Should I {action1} or {action2}?"
- missing_target: "Where should I place it? Bin A or Bin B?"
- same_type_multiple: "I see {count} {object}s. Which one: the one on the left or the right?"

## QUERY RESPONSE TEMPLATES
- scene_description: "I see {description} on the table."
- count_response: "There are {count} {object}(s) on the table."
- count_single: "There is 1 {object} on the table."
- count_none: "There are no {object}s on the table."
- recommendation: "Based on what I see, I recommend {suggestion}."

## STATUS TEMPLATES
- ready: "Ready. What would you like me to do?"
- busy: "I am currently {action}. Please wait."
- progress: "Sorting in progress: {done} of {total} objects sorted."
